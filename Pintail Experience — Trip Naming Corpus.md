# Pintail Trip Naming Corpus

**Purpose:** the raw material and rules for naming Pintail hunts/trips. Faith + place + grit, understated. Built from research on scripture themes, waterfowl/hunting lingo, and Mississippi-flyway/Tennessee nature. Feeds the `pintail-trip-namer` skill.

---

## The register (read this first)

The audience is faith-driven but understated hunters. They respond to authenticity, place, tradition, and quiet conviction — not marketing gloss or a church-camp theme. Every name must earn its keep against three tests:

1. **Say-it-out-loud test.** It works in *"I'm doing ___ with Pintail"* or *"the ___ hunt."* One or two words. If a guy wouldn't say it in a truck, cut it.
2. **One quiet faith note, max.** Weave faith in through imagery that's *already* about water, wings, light, and provision — not stacked Psalm puns. Subtlety reads as conviction; overreach reads as precious.
3. **Concrete over abstract, rugged over polished.** "Flooded Timber," "First Light," "Frost" beat "Blessing," "Faithful," "Sanctuary." Regional specificity (Reelfoot, cypress, New Madrid, the bottoms) earns credibility.

**Words to use sparingly / avoid** (too soft or churchy unless anchored to place or grit): sanctuary, blessing, worship, glory, faithful, journey, and — per Isaac — "curated." "Refuge" is borderline; only use it if it's clearly doing double duty (wildlife refuge + Psalm 46).

**Naming vs. the parent brand.** "The Pintail Experience" / "Pintail" is the umbrella. Individual events are named hunts/trips referred to as *hunts* or *trips* (interchangeable — leaves room for non-hunting trips later). Format in-app: **Name · Place · Dates · Length/Type** (e.g., *"First Light · Reelfoot, TN · Dec 30–Jan 3 · 4-day duck"*).

## Theme families (faith ∩ nature/hunting — the double-duty core)

Each family is a place where scripture imagery and duck-hunting reality already overlap. This overlap is the whole trick.

**Water & flood** — Ps 23 "beside still waters," Luke 5 "launch out into the deep," Isaiah "water in the wilderness." Hunt reality: flooded timber, backwater, sloughs, the flats, the flooded flats, predawn boat ride.
*Words:* Still Water, Deep Water, Backwater, The Flats, High Water, Floodwater, The Slough.

**Light & dawn** — dawn as God's faithfulness ("as surely as the arrival of dawn"), morning star, light in darkness. Hunt reality: the predawn ride, shooting light, first flight at gray light.
*Words:* First Light, Daybreak, Gray Light, Morning Star, Shooting Light, The Dawn, Break of Day.

**Wings, flight & migration** — "under his wings" (hen/dove/eagle), Jer 8:7 "even the stork knows her appointed seasons; the dove, the swift and the thrush observe their migration." Hunt reality: the flyway, the migration, the grand passage, cupped and committed, locked up.
*Words:* Grand Passage, The Migration, The Flyway, Appointed Season, Under Wing, On the Wing, The Passage.

**Refuge & shelter** — Ps 46 "God is our refuge," the rock, the fortress. Hunt reality: the blind, the wildlife refuge, the harbor, out of the wind.
*Words:* The Refuge, The Rock, The Blind, Harbor, Stronghold, The Lee (out of the wind).

**Provision & providence** — ravens feed Elijah, quail in the desert, daily bread, "the earth is the Lord's." Hunt reality: the take, the limit, the harvest, meat in the freezer, market-hunting heritage.
*Words:* Providence, The Harvest, Daily Bread, The Provision, The Take.

**Wilderness & the wild** — the wilderness as testing, meeting God, and refuge; "the wilderness shall rejoice" (Isa 35). Hunt reality: the bottoms, the timber, the marsh, the backcountry, the cane.
*Words:* The Wilderness, The Bottoms, The Timber, The Marsh, Cane Ridge, Backcountry.

**Gathering & fellowship** — Heb 10 "do not forsake the gathering," the table, breaking bread. Hunt reality: duck camp, the fire, the table, the lodge.
*Words:* The Gathering (soft — use with a place), The Table, Fireside, Duck Camp, The Fold.

**Call & voice** — the Shepherd's voice, "my sheep hear my voice," the call to follow. Hunt reality: the duck call, calling ducks, the hail call. (Rich double meaning.)
*Words:* The Call, The Hail, Answered Call.

**Legacy & generations** — heritage, fathers and sons, "tell it to your children," the Reelfoot third-generation-guide tradition, market-hunting history.
*Words:* Heritage, The Old Ways, Generations, Ebenezer ("stone of help," 1 Sam 7:12 — a remembrance stone).

**Steadfastness & the remnant** — the rock, the anchor, the cornerstone, the faithful remnant; the last flight of the day.
*Words:* Steadfast, The Anchor, The Remnant (also: the last flight), Cornerstone, Selah (pause/reflect).

## Term banks

**Waterfowl of the Mississippi flyway / Reelfoot** (the pintail is the brand — and a Reelfoot species): pintail (sprig), mallard (greenhead), gadwall (gray duck), wigeon, blue-winged & green-winged teal, wood duck (woodie), canvasback ("cans," the king of ducks), bufflehead ("buffies"), merganser, redhead, black duck, Canada goose, specklebelly, snow goose.

**Hunt nouns & phrases:** blind, decoy / block / deke, spread / rig / set, spinner / mojo, timber, flooded timber, slough, backwater, oxbow, the flats, the bottoms, honey hole, flyway, limit, the take, first light, shooting light, locked up, cupped, committed, feet down, the call, the hail call, greenheads, retriever, the boat ride, the freeze, bluebird day.

**Nature & regional (West TN / NW flyway):** cypress, tupelo, cane, Reelfoot, New Madrid, the river bottoms, the Mississippi, oxbow, backwater, frost, fog, gray light, north wind, the freeze, flooded rice, green timber.

**Faith words that survive the register** (concrete or storied, not soft): Providence, Refuge (with place), Remnant, Ebenezer, Steadfast, Covenant, Dominion, Harvest, Redemption, Deliverance, Exodus, Selah, Hallowed, The Fold, The Vine, Firstfruits, Promise.

## Naming patterns

1. **Place-anchored** — `[Place] + element/virtue`: *Reelfoot Dawn, Cypress & Cross, New Madrid, Cane Ridge.* Highest credibility with hunters.
2. **Single strong word** (only if rugged/ownable): *Providence, Remnant, Ebenezer, Steadfast, Selah.*
3. **Two-word, concrete noun/adj**: *First Light, Grand Passage, Flooded Timber, Deep Water, Gray Light, High Water.*
4. **Double-duty (faith ∩ hunt)** — one word/phrase carrying both: *The Call, The Passage, Under Wing, The Remnant, Appointed Season, The Harvest.*

Keep a series coherent: pick a lane per season or rotate deliberately (e.g., a "light" name, a "water" name, a "place" name), so the set feels curated-by-hand, not random.

## Green-light shortlist (passes all three tests)

| Name | Faith note | Hunt note | Says well? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **First Light** | dawn = God's faithfulness | the predawn flight | "doing First Light" ✅ |
| **Grand Passage** | pilgrimage / passage | the great migration term | "the Grand Passage hunt" ✅ |
| **Providence** | divine provision | the birds that show | "going to Providence" ✅ |
| **Flooded Timber** | — (pure grit) | classic green-timber hunt | "Flooded Timber" ✅ |
| **The Passage** | scripture passage / pilgrimage | migration | ✅ |
| **The Remnant** | the faithful remnant | the last flight of the day | ✅ |
| **Ebenezer** | "stone of help" (1 Sam 7:12) | a remembrance-stone story name | distinctive, needs the story |
| **Cypress & Cross** | the Cross | West TN cypress bottoms | Isaac liked; location-anchored ✅ |
| **The Call** | the Shepherd's voice | the duck call | ✅ |
| **Reelfoot Dawn** | dawn | the lake itself | ✅ |

**Cut (too soft / churchy for the register):** Still Waters, The Refuge (unless place-anchored), The Gathering, Deep Water-as-solo, Sanctuary, anything with "Blessing/Faithful/Journey," heavy alliteration ("Frost & Fellowship").
